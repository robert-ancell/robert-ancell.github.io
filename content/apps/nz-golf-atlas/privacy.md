*Last updated: 14 August 2026*

**Short version: the app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking, and it never sends your location anywhere.**

## What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers about you is kept on your phone and nowhere
else:

- the courses you have starred, opened recently, or unlocked
- the rounds you have kept, with the players' names and their scores
- whether you have upgraded
- any problem report you have saved

None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, or readable by anyone but you.
Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy on a server, because
there is no server.

## Your location

The app can show where you are on the course, which way you are facing,
and how far you are from the green. All of that is worked out on your
device.

**Your position is never transmitted.** It is not sent to us, and not
sent to any other service. If you decline the location permission, or
turn it off later, everything else in the app still works — you simply do
not get the dot or the distances.

## The weather

When you open a course's details, the app asks the Norwegian
Meteorological Institute for the forecast. To do that it sends **the
coordinates of the golf course** — a public place, listed in the app for
everyone — together with the app's name and version. It does not send
your position, an identifier, or anything about you. Their terms are at
[api.met.no](https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService).

This is the only request the app makes on its own. Everything else it
draws is in the app already, which is why it works with no signal.

## Reporting a problem with a map

If you report a problem with a course, the app prepares an email for you
containing the course's name, what you ticked, anything you wrote, and
the drawing if you made one. **It is your email app that sends it**, and
nothing is sent until you press send. A copy is saved on your device.

Reports are read only to fix the maps. They are not published, shared, or
used for anything else.

## Links out of the app

Directions, a club's website, and the licence pages open in your maps app
or browser. Once you are there, that app's and that website's own privacy
terms apply, not this one.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not by this app.
They tell the app only whether a purchase exists; the app never sees your
name, your email, or your payment details, and no purchase information is
sent anywhere else.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Your rights

There is nothing held about you to see, correct, export or delete —
everything is on your own device, under your own control. If you have
sent a problem report by email and would like it deleted, ask at the
address below and it will be.

## Changes

If the app ever collects anything, this policy will say so before that
version ships, and the date at the top will change.
