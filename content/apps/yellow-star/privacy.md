*Last updated: 14 August 2026*

**Yellow Star collects no information about you, and never sends
anything anywhere.**

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
crash reporting. The app has no networking code in it, so it cannot
contact a server even in principle — including this one.

Nothing is kept about you either. The app remembers nothing between
runs, so there is nothing held to see, correct, export or delete.

The app is not directed at children, and collects nothing from anyone.

If a future version ever changes any of this, this policy will say so
before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
