**A yellow star. Tap it and see what it does.**

It might spin, pop like a balloon, grow into a flower, vanish under falling
snow or shower itself in confetti. There are 50 unique animations, and another
one every time you tap.

Keep tapping to see them all. Bright, smooth and full of surprises, it is a
fun distraction for a spare minute, and suitable for children of all ages.
